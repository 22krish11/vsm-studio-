Other companies start with gear. We start with finding the right story to tell and why.

01

Discovery & Storyfinding

We uncover what others walk right past. Through discovery conversations that are more like an investigative pursuit rather than a standard checklist, we extract the hidden narrative treasure that was waiting to be found.

02

Story Strategy

We establish five core keywords serving as your foundational story blueprint. We pick the right character, develop an impactful plot and pitch you on the plan. We collaborate together to refine the story strategy.

03

Filming

We get to work on all the logistics, crew, locations, rentals and scheduling needed for the production. Our team of professionals will be ready to craft the vision we planned out. We film with intention it's not a guessing game.

Strategic Distribution

What happens on this call?

You’ll meet with Evan for 20 minutes to discuss your company, your brand, and your vision. Having worked with big and small brands alike, we know exactly what it takes to drive meaningful story, BUT make it results-driven. 

That’s a difficult merge to create in the real world, but we’ve mastered that science of story and results. 

For your company, ideally that means more sales, more referrals, and more repeat customers. 

So you’ll meet with Evan and build out a strategy plan. 

If it makes sense for us to proceed further and bring your vision to reality, then we’ll build something together. 

How much does it cost?

Our rates start at $5,000 per month, which includes a comprehensive package of strategy, creative development, film production, and distribution. This foundational investment ensures that you receive top-tier service and impactful content creation tailored to your brand’s unique story and goals.

However, the ultimate budget will depend on the specific marketing channels you wish to focus on and the intensity of your growth ambitions. During your call with Evan, she will delve into your objectives and desired outcomes, whether that’s growing your email list, expanding your social following, or running targeted ads to drive sales of a new product.

Based on this conversation, we’ll present you with tiered options for our services. These options will vary depending on the number of channels and the scope of your campaign. We design our packages to be flexible, allowing you to scale up or down according to your needs and the results you’re seeing.

Our goal is to create a partnership that aligns perfectly with your vision and delivers measurable growth. Let’s explore how we can help you achieve your business goals with creative storytelling and strategic execution.

Are there any contracts or commitments?

If you do a call with Evan and we both feel that it’s a great fit, we’d be thrilled to embark on this journey together.

We pride ourselves on being a dedicated, small team that offers unparalleled, personalized service. Our aim is to build long-lasting partnerships that drive remarkable results and transformative growth for your brand.

However, we understand the importance of flexibility and earning your trust. That’s why we don’t lock you into any long-term commitments. We begin with a 3-month retainer, during which we dive into Storyfinding, content development, and strategic distribution.

After these initial three months, we transition to a month-to-month agreement. If, at any point, you feel that our partnership isn’t delivering the desired returns, all we ask is for a 30-day notice. This approach ensures that you have the freedom and confidence to continue only if you see the tangible impact of our collaboration.
